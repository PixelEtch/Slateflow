# Code of Conduct

Slateflow is a small project, and we want everyone who uses it or contributes to it to feel welcome. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intent; ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about any person or group.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly ignoring a request to stop.

## Reporting

Email **thepixeletch@gmail.com** with what happened, where, and links or screenshots if you have them. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept confidential. If the report is about the maintainer, say so and it will be handled as fairly as one person can.

For security problems, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and temporarily or permanently block anyone who breaks these rules. Usually the first step is a private warning; serious or repeated cases skip it.
